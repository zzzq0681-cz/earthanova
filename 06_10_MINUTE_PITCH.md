# 06 — 10-Minute Pitch & Live Demo Script

**Aim:** Show one clear, memorable experience—not a full product roadmap.  
**Max duration:** 10 minutes.  
**Demo scenario:** Alexandria, Egypt (synthetic values).

## Timeboxed presentation

| Time | Segment | What to say / show |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:45 | Hook | “What if you could click anywhere on Earth and immediately explore how that place is changing?” |
| 0:45–1:30 | Problem | Environmental data is fragmented and difficult to interpret, especially for non-specialists. |
| 1:30–2:15 | Solution | Earth Sentinel combines a 3D globe with a compact change-analysis command center. |
| 2:15–5:45 | **Live demo** | Rotate Earth → choose Alexandria → popup → Scan Area → compare change → scenario → station highlight. |
| 5:45–7:00 | How it works | Geographic selection + data workflow + visualized findings. Explain that demo data are synthetic. |
| 7:00–8:00 | Value / users | Citizens, researchers, planners. Say how the concept could become SaaS later. |
| 8:00–9:00 | Next step | Replace synthetic data with NASA sources; validate trends and analysis methods. |
| 9:00–10:00 | Final takeaway + buffer | One-click planetary exploration → understandable change story. Leave seconds for glitches/questions. |

## Exact live click path
1. Open demo; show full rotating planet for a few seconds.
2. Drag globe and zoom briefly to prove it is interactive.
3. Choose **Alexandria, Egypt** from globe or preset.
4. Point out location popup and latitude/longitude.
5. Click **Scan Area**. Explain that animation visualizes the analysis workflow.
6. Show **Before vs After**. Say “in our *simulated case*, temperature rose by 1.4°C and rainfall fell by 8%.”
7. Show **Key Changes** and the short finding.
8. Toggle **Baseline → Stress Case** on the **illustrative** 5-year scenario.
9. Click one virtual tower. Explain it represents a data stream, not a physical tower.
10. End on the full dashboard.

## Sample speaking script (Arabic Egyptian)
> "تخيل إنك عايز تعرف منطقة على الأرض اتغيرت إزاي، من غير ما تتعامل مع ملفات وأرقام معقدة. Earth Sentinel بيخليك تختار المكان مباشرة من كرة أرضية تفاعلية. أول ما نضغط على الإسكندرية، بنشوف الإحداثيات ونبدأ فحص بصري. هنا عندنا داتا تجريبية للعرض: بنقارن الفترة السابقة بالحالية، ونظهر أهم التغيرات بوضوح. نقدر كمان نشوف سيناريوهات توضيحية للسنين الجاية، وأربع محطات افتراضية بتوضح أنواع البيانات اللي ممكن نراقبها. إحنا النهارده بنعرض الكونسبت وتجربة الاستخدام؛ الخطوة اللي بعد كده هي ربط بيانات ناسا الفعلية والتحقق العلمي من النتائج."

## What NOT to say
- “We monitor every city in real time.”
- “This is a statistically proven climate trend.”
- “Our model predicts the next 5 years accurately.”
- “These four towers are real physical sensors.”
- “This area is safe for families / guaranteed for investment.”

## Closing line
> “Earth Sentinel makes Earth observation explorable. Today we demonstrate the experience; next we connect validated science.”

## Backup if globe interaction fails
Use the Alexandria preset button and continue the story. Have a short screen recording or screenshots ready. The pitch should not depend on network calls succeeding on stage.
